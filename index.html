Chapter 01 — Club Identity

Victoria Concordia Crescit

Founded in 1886 by munitions workers in South East London, Arsenal Football Club has grown into one of the most iconic institutions in world football. Guided by its Latin motto, "Victory Through Harmony", the club revolutionized English football through tactical innovation, attacking flair, and an unmatched 14 FA Cup triumphs.

“We don't buy superstars, we make them.”

— Arsène Wenger, Manager (1996–2018)

Woolwich (The Birthplace — 1886)

Arsenal's story began at the Royal Arsenal armaments factory in Woolwich as "Dial Square." In 1893, they became the first club from Southern England to join the Football League.

Highbury Stadium (1913–2006)

Moving north across the Thames in 1913, Highbury became Arsenal's spiritual home for 93 years, witnessing the Herbert Chapman revolution and the historic 2003–04 "Invincibles" season.

51.5577° N, 0.1029° W

Emirates Stadium (2006–Present)

Opened in July 2006 with a capacity of over 60,700 seats, the Emirates Stadium stands just 500 yards from Highbury, symbolizing Arsenal's modern European stature.

Chapter 03 — Chronology

Historical Milestones

1886

Dial Square is Born

Workers at the Royal Arsenal in Woolwich establish the club, playing their first match against Eastern Wanderers on December 11, 1886.

1925–1934

The Herbert Chapman Revolution

Manager Herbert Chapman introduces the famous WM formation, numbered shirts, floodlights, and secures Arsenal's first major league and cup trophies.

1970–1971

The First League & Cup Double

Under Bertie Mee, Arsenal clinch their first European trophy (Inter-Cities Fairs Cup) followed by a historic First Division and FA Cup Double at White Hart Lane.

2003–2004

The Invincibles

Managed by Arsène Wenger and led by Thierry Henry, Arsenal complete an entire 38-game Premier League season unbeaten (26 wins, 12 draws), earning a unique Golden Trophy.

2020–PRESENT

Mikel Arteta's Renaissance

Former club captain Mikel Arteta revitalizes the squad with a young core, lifting the 14th FA Cup and re-establishing Arsenal as consistent title and Champions League contenders.
